Why this exists

We remember week one.

You arrived at Bunda not knowing where anything is. The lecture halls are full. The content moves fast. You're trying to make friends, understand your timetable, find notes, and not fall behind — all at the same time.

Nobody handed you a map. So we built one.

📚

The notes are real

Every quiz is built from actual LUANAR lecture slides and past papers. Not random internet questions.

🏆

You compete with peers

A leaderboard tracks scores across the group. Healthy competition makes revision feel less like suffering.

💬

Discussion per question

Every question has a comment section. When something confuses you, someone in the group likely knows.

⚡

Explanations that teach

Getting an answer wrong teaches you more than getting it right. Every explanation comes from the lecture content itself.

How it works

Three things. Simple.

01

A quiz drops in the group

Every week or two a quiz link appears in ONE % Study Hub. You open it in your phone browser — no app needed, no login, no cost.

02

You register your name and attempt it

Enter your name and reg number. Answer the questions. Each answer shows you exactly why it is right or wrong — from your actual notes.

03

Your score goes on the leaderboard

Your result is saved. You can retry, improve, and climb the board. The group discusses. Everyone gets better together.

Community

One group. One standard.

ONE % Study Hub works because everyone in it takes it seriously. A few things that keep it that way.

The house rules

✅
Attempt every quiz genuinely. Your score is your measure, not your reputation. Nobody is judging.
💬
Discuss, don't just post answers. The comment section under each question is for understanding — use it.
🤝
Help when you know. If you understand a concept someone is struggling with, say so. That is what ONE % means.
🔇
No spam, no noise. The group is for study content only. Keep it clean.
🔄
Retry until you pass. Every quiz can be retaken. The goal is understanding, not a one-time score.
